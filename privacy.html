Privacy Policy (GDPR)

This policy describes how Aura HomeSystems (Petrov Solutions) collects, uses, and protects your personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

Last updated: 27 March 2026

1. Data controller

Petrov Solutions, Bulgaria, Varna 9000

Contact email: office@aurahomesystems.eu

2. What data we collect

2.1. Data on registration and login

  • email
  • password (hashed, not stored in plain text)

2.2. Data when using the service

  • sensor status (open/closed)
  • event history
  • account settings
  • information on devices used to log in (for the 3-device limit)

2.3. Data on order or inquiry

  • name or nickname
  • email
  • phone
  • delivery address
  • message content

2.4. Financial data

When paying via Revolut, bank card (e.g. Stripe), or another licensed provider, Aura HomeSystems does not collect or store full card data (number, CVC). Such data is processed directly by the relevant payment provider (e.g. Revolut Business, Stripe) in accordance with their security policies.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Data is processed for:

  • Providing the service (account, dashboard, push notifications). Legal basis: contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Push notifications are an essential part of the service; the legal basis for sending them is contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • System security and device limit. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
  • Order processing and delivery. Legal basis: contract performance
  • Responding to inquiries. Legal basis: consent and/or pre-contractual steps

We do not carry out profiling or automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

4. Storage and transfer of data

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers in the EU.

Firebase (Google) acts as a processor of personal data within the meaning of Art. 28 GDPR. Google provides standard contractual clauses and a data processing agreement (DPA) that ensure GDPR compliance.

Passwords are stored only as a cryptographic hash.

Form data may be transferred to email service providers (Formspree, Resend) solely for sending messages.

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Retention periods

Account data is kept while the account is active.

After account deletion, data is deleted or anonymised within the periods required by law.

When you delete your account, we delete your account and sensor data from our systems (including Firebase). Backups may retain data for a limited period until overwritten. You may also request erasure ("right to be forgotten") by email above.

Delete your account online (including dashboard data): Account deletion page.

Data deletion

Users may delete their account and all related data via the Settings menu in the mobile app, via the account deletion page on our website, or by requesting deletion at office@aurahomesystems.eu. After confirmation, the account and sensor data are permanently erased from our active databases within 30 days.

Order and accounting data is retained in accordance with legal retention periods (e.g. 5–10 years).

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access to your data
  • rectification
  • erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • restriction of processing
  • data portability
  • object
  • withdraw consent

You can exercise your rights by email: office@aurahomesystems.eu

www.cpdp.bg.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use localStorage for:

  • language preference
  • device identifier on login

These data are technically necessary for the functioning of the service and the security of the account.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or trackers.

8. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect data as required by Art. 32 GDPR. Technical measures include:

  • encryption of data in transit and at rest (where applicable)
  • secure connections (HTTPS) for all web access
  • storage of passwords only as a cryptographic hash (not in plain text)
  • restricted access to data for authorised personnel and systems only
  • security logging and monitoring for unauthorised access

Organisational measures include processing agreements with providers (e.g. Firebase) and internal procedures for access and confidentiality.

9. Changes to the policy

Updates will be published on this page.

We will notify users of material changes where applicable.
