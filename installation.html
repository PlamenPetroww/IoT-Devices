How to install smart door and window sensors

Aura HomeSystems is a smart home solution with wireless sensors for entrance doors and windows. After setup you get phone alerts on every opening – ideal when you're away from home.

1. Register in the app

Install Aura HomeSystems from Google Play or open the site on your phone. Register with email and password – use the same email when configuring the sensor.

2. Mount the sensor

Place the sensor on the door or window: one part on the fixed frame, the other on the moving part. When closed, the magnets should be close together.

3. WiFi setup (2.4 GHz)

Power on the sensor and connect to AuraHomeSystems_Setup. In the portal enter your home WiFi (2.4 GHz), password, email and sensor name (e.g. "Front door").

4. Check the dashboard

Sign in to the dashboard – the sensor should appear automatically. Open and close the door/window and watch live status.

5. Push notifications

Enable Away mode. In phone settings (Apps → Aura HomeSystems): turn Notifications on and set Battery to Unrestricted / Don't optimize — otherwise alerts may delay in standby. Confirm notifications are allowed before testing. You get an alert immediately when something opens.
